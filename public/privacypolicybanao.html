Strict Privacy & Security Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Honest Data Ownership (Zero Vendor Lock-in)

FormForge is an open-source form backend designed to put you in complete control of your data. We do not operate a hosted SaaS, meaning 100% of your submission data, database records, and logs are kept inside your own Cloudflare D1 account. We have zero access to your database, user profiles, or submission details.

2. Military-Grade Cryptographic Encryption

To secure SMTP servers and Turnstile bot protection secrets, FormForge implements automated data encryption:

  • AES-GCM (256-bit) Encryption: Your SMTP email passwords and Cloudflare Turnstile Secret keys are fully encrypted in the SQLite D1 database using the edge-native Web Crypto API.
  • Secret-Key Authentication: Encryption keys are derived directly from your environment variable AUTH_SECRET.
  • Masked Retrieval: To prevent credential leaks in API responses or dashboard screens, both passwords and Turnstile secret keys are completely stripped and replaced with placeholders (__SMTP_PASSWORD_SET__ and __TURNSTILE_SECRET_SET__) before transit.

3. Privacy-First IP & Metadata Handling

To defend against spambots while maintaining compliance with strict global privacy laws (such as GDPR and CCPA):

  • IP Hashing: We do not store raw IP addresses. If enabled in configuration, submission IP addresses are instantly hashed using SHA-256 with a unique form salt.
  • Zero Third-Party Analytics: FormForge has no Google Analytics, no trackers, and no external tracking pixels. Your dashboard telemetry is entirely self-hosted.

4. Smart SSRF Webhook Protections

FormForge handles outgoing notification alerts (Slack, Discord, MS Teams, Mattermost, etc.) directly from your worker. To block malicious requests targeting your internal networks, we strictly reject any webhook destination resolved to private, local, or loopback networks (e.g., localhost, 127.0.0.1, 10.0.0.0/8, 192.168.0.0/16).

5. Outbox Task Isolation

Email delivery and Webhook alerts are dispatched asynchronously in background thread queues using Cloudflare's non-blocking waitUntil tasks. This ensures your clients experience immediate submission speeds (typically < 15ms) while ensuring failure of any external notification API never blocks your core sqlite data writes.
